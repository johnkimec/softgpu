softgpu

A driver stack, measured on a 6-vCPU ARM VM

From a mailbox and two spinning cores to a char device.

softgpu is a software model of a GPU. The device is threads and memcpy on a 1 GHz clock. The stack above it is the real subject: rings, fences, interrupts, a page table, a runlist, and the user/kernel boundary those things sit on.

Ten stages, in the order they were built.

Each one started from a number in the previous record. The stage ids follow the design notes, which is why 7 landed before 5.

What idle costs.

One 64-byte fill after a quiet gap. spin keeps both engines hot. sleep gates immediately and pays the wake on every command. Hybrid 50 µs is the default: gaps inside the budget are free, and a quiet device drops to a few hundredths of a core.

    Latency of the command that breaks the gap. Log scale. A 10 µs budget gates on the 30 µs row. 50 µs and 200 µs both wait until 300 µs; the longer budget does not make that command faster. Source: gate-spin.json, gated.json, gate-sleep.json, vm7-idle10k.json, vm7-idle200k.json.
